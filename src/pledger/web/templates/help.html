Pledger is a personal ledger: a list of every time money moved, where it came from and where it went. From that list it works out how much you have in each account, how much you earned and spent, and how your wealth changes over time.

Each time money moves you add one movement: a date, what kind of movement it was, the two accounts involved and the amount. Everything else in Pledger is calculated from those movements.

💱 All amounts are in your base currency ({{ settings.currency }}). If you hold other currencies, enter their value converted to the base currency.

👁️ Showing Pledger to someone? The eye button at the top right replaces every amount with ****** until you click it again.

🏦 Accounts

An account is any place where money is kept or owed. There are three kinds:

  • Assets: what you own, such as cash, a bank account or investments.
  • Liabilities: what you owe, such as a credit card balance or a debt.
  • Equity: your net worth, that is, assets minus liabilities. There is a single equity account, and Pledger uses it on its own for gains and losses.

⚖️ Debit and credit

Every movement has two sides, and the amount is the same on both:

  • The debit account is where the money goes to ➡️.
  • The credit account is where the money comes from ⬅️.

For a liability this works the other way round from what you might expect: paying off a credit card debits the card, because you now owe less, and buying with the card credits it, because you now owe more.

🔀 Movement types

  • G Gain: money that makes you richer, such as a salary or a gift. It is credited to equity.
  • L Loss: money that makes you poorer, such as food, rent or fees. It is debited from equity.
  • T Transfer: money that moves between your own accounts, such as a cash withdrawal or a credit card payment. It does not change your equity.

🏷️ Base movements

A base movement is a category for movements, such as "Basic - Food" or "Work - Salary Main". Choosing one tells Pledger whether the movement is a gain, a loss or a transfer. Some base movements always use the same account on one side: in the form that side is filled in for you and cannot be changed. A side shown as - means any account can be used there.

🗂️ Result types

Result types group gains and losses into larger families, such as Operative (everyday income and spending), Investments or Taxes. Every gain and loss base movement belongs to exactly one result type; transfers have none. Result types are numbered, and the Charts tab lists them in that order, with a running total that shows how each family adds to or takes from your profit.

✍️ Record a movement

  1. Go to Ledger.
  2. Pick the date and the type: gain, loss or transfer.
  3. Pick the base movement that best describes it.
  4. Choose the debit and credit accounts, unless they are already filled in.
  5. Enter the amount, add a comment if you like, and press Add movement.

If something does not fit, for example an account that cannot be used with that base movement, Pledger explains the problem and nothing is saved.

🩹 Fix a mistake

Movements cannot be edited. Delete the wrong one with the × button at the end of its row and add it again with the right details.

➕ Add an account

Go to Accounts, fill in the New account form (name, type and description) and press Add account.

➕ Add a result type or base movement

Go to Setup, open New result type or New base movement, fill in the form and press the button. Every entry needs a short name and a description.

  • A new base movement that is a gain or a loss also needs a result type.
  • For a base movement's debit and credit, choose an account to always use that account, or leave - to choose the account each time.
  • A new result type is added at the end of the list.

To rename, reorder or remove entries, the settings files have to be edited by hand.

🔎 Look at a single month or account

Use the Month filter on Ledger, Accounts or Charts (it is remembered across tabs), and the Account filter on Ledger. Pick All time to go back.

🤔 Why does my profit differ from the change in my bank balance?

Profit only counts gains and losses. Transfers, such as moving money to savings or paying a credit card bill, change account balances but not your profit.

🔒 Why can't I choose the equity account?

Pledger uses equity on its own: it is the credit side of every gain and the debit side of every loss, and transfers never touch it.

⚠️ The page says it could not load the ledger.

One of the settings files or the ledger has a problem, for example a movement that uses an account or base movement that no longer exists. The message says what is wrong; fix that entry in the file and reload the page.
