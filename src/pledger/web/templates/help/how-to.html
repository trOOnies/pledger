🩹 Fix a mistake

Click the ✎ button at the end of the movement's row in the tab that added it: the form is filled in with it. Fix it and press Save changes, or Cancel. It keeps its ID. To remove it instead, use the × button.

  • Gains, losses and transfers: in the Ledger.
  • The rest, in their own tab, which the colored circle of their row in the Ledger links to: the investment movements in Investments, the conversions in Currencies, the debt movements in People, the card payments in Credit Cards and the products of a cart in Cart.
  • The ones pledger generates (salaries, services, initial amounts, revaluations) change with what they come from: edit the month in Work, the value in Services, the account in Accounts.

🤝 Keep track of who owes you, and whom you owe

Each kind of creditor has its tab and its accounts:

  • People: lending, borrowing and paying back, from the People tab, picking who it is about. Debts go to a debt account and loans to a loan account, of the currency they are in (each currency has both, in the currencies file). Move debt between two of them, or two people, with the Debt/loan exchange form, and to another currency with Change debt/loan currency.
  • Services: pay them from the Services tab.
  • Companies: add the salaries you receive from the Work tab.

For other movements of a debt account, write the person's, service's or company's name in the comment, such as "Lunch with Dad". Each tab adds them to them, and the People tab warns you about movements that name no one.

🔎 Look at a single month or account

Use the Month filter on Ledger, Accounts, Results, People or Credit Cards (it is remembered across tabs), and the Account filter on Ledger. Pick All time to go back.
