✍️ Record a movement

  1. Go to Ledger.
  2. Pick the date and the type: gain, loss or transfer.
  3. Pick the base movement that best describes it.
  4. Choose the debit and credit accounts, unless they are already filled in.
  5. Enter the amount, add a comment if you like, and press Add movement.

Bought with a credit card as the credit account? An Installments field shows up: leave it empty for a single payment, or write how many monthly installments it is in. The card is charged the whole amount that day all the same; the Credit Cards tab spreads what is paid of it, one installment a month.

If something does not fit, for example an account that cannot be used with that base movement, Pledger explains the problem and nothing is saved.

⧉ Repeat a movement

Click the ⧉ button at the end of the movement's row in the Ledger: the New movement form is filled in with it, date included. Change what you need, usually the date, and press Add movement. The original stays as it was, and the copy gets its own ID.

🩹 Fix a mistake

Click the ✎ button at the end of the movement's row in the Ledger: the form becomes Edit movement, filled in with it. Fix it and press Save changes, or Cancel to go back to a new movement. It keeps its ID. To remove it instead, use the × button. The movements of other tabs are edited the same way there: the investment movements in Investments, the conversions in Currencies, the debt movements in Debt and the card payments in Credit Cards. The ones pledger generates (salaries, initial amounts, revaluations) change with what they come from.

➕ Add an account

Go to Accounts, fill in the New account form (name, type and description) and press Add account.

Optionally, pick a check on its balance at the end of each day: Net positive (never negative) or Net zero (always 0.00, for an intermediary account). Movements that would break it are rejected, and the Checks column of the balances shows the accounts that have one.

An account that earns or pays interest on money needs its interest frequency: weekly or monthly. There is none by default, and only the accounts with one can have interest on money: the Investments tab lists them in its Interest on money section and warns about each past week, or month, without a movement of interest of them.

🧭 Which kind of account?

Before adding an account, follow the questions from the top: the first Yes tells you what to use. Some answers are not a new account at all, but an entry of an account that already exists.

Is it something you own, or something you owe?

  1. 🟦 You own it: an asset
  2. Does it hold investments with a quantity and a price (bonds, stocks, funds, fixed terms…)? Yes → an investment account: an asset listed in investment.yaml, with its investments. Its initial amount comes from them.
  3. Is it money that someone owes you (a loan you gave)? Yes → no new account: add them to creditors.yaml, with a negative amount, and use the Debt tab: loans are in Lo, the loan account.
  4. Is it only a step between movements that always goes back to 0.00? Yes → no new account: use Fi, the intermediary (net zero). Add one of your own only if you need it apart, with the Net zero check.
  5. None of the above (cash, a bank or wallet balance, a safe box…) → a regular asset, from the Accounts tab. Then: its currency, if not the base one; Liquid if it can be spent right away; Net positive if it can never go below 0.00; and its interest frequency if it earns interest.
  1. 🟧 You owe it: a liability
  2. Is it a credit card? Yes → a credit card account, in credit_cards.yaml: always liquid, paid from the Credit Cards tab, and its purchases can be in installments.
  3. Is it owed to a person or a service (a friend, the rent, a bill)? Yes → no new account: add them to creditors.yaml, with a positive amount, and use the Debt tab: debts are in De, the debt account. Your employers are there already, from the Work tab, in Lo.
  4. None of the above (a bank loan, a mortgage…) → a regular liability, from the Accounts tab, with its currency and Liquid if it is due right away.

There is no new equity account: Eq is the only one, and pledger uses it for every gain and loss.

➕ Add a result type or base movement

Go to Results, open New result type or New base movement, fill in the form and press the button. Every entry needs a short name and a description.

  • A new base movement that is a gain or a loss also needs a result type.
  • For a base movement's debit and credit, choose an account to always use that account, or leave - to choose the account each time.
  • A new result type is added at the end of the list.

To rename, reorder or remove entries, the settings files have to be edited by hand.

📈 Buy or sell an investment

Go to Investments and use its form, not the Ledger one. Name the investment and, when buying, selling or splitting, its quantity. The money comes from (or goes to) another account, such as your bank account. Interest, dividends and broker fees are recorded there too. When buying or selling, or for a dividend, fill in Broker fees to add them in one go. The Currency follows the account you pick: Pledger handles the conversion for you.

🤝 Keep track of who owes you

Add lending, borrowing and paying back from the Debt tab, picking who it is about: debts go to De and loans to Lo. For other movements of a debt account, write the person's, service's or company's name in the comment, such as "Lunch with Dad". The Debt tab adds them to them, and warns you about movements that name no one.

🔍 Check your balances against reality

Go to Audit and fill in the balances you see today, leaving empty the accounts you don't want to check. pledger hides its own until you confirm. If one differs, find the mistake and fix it, or use the button next to it to add a correction dated today.

🔎 Look at a single month or account

Use the Month filter on Ledger, Accounts, Results, Debt or Credit Cards (it is remembered across tabs), and the Account filter on Ledger. Pick All time to go back.
