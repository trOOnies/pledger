Enter a whole purchase at once, instead of one movement per product in the Ledger.

  • A cart has a date, a store and the account it is paid from (in your base currency; a credit card too).
  • Its products go in bags. Each bag has a loss base movement and, paid with a credit card, a number of installments, shared by its products. Two bags may have the same ones, as bags only organize the cart.
  • Each product is a movement of the ledger, its comment naming the store, then the product. The Ledger tab shows them with a 🟤.

The carts are listed here, and are edited (keeping the IDs of the products kept) and deleted, with their movements, only from this tab.
