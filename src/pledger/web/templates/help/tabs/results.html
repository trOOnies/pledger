How much your equity changed in the period, where it came from, and how long it would last. The gains and losses are grouped by result type.

📏 Change of equity and months of expenses

How much your equity changed, nominal and real (taking out inflation), and how many months of expenses it covers: how long your equity would last spending what you spent on average each month of the last year.

Expenses are your losses, leaving out the initial movements, the built-in result types and the result types tagged Employment (see the glossary): if you lost your job, those would stop too.

🗂️ By result type

  • Your gains and losses grouped by result type: click a result type to see what is inside it, and tick Cumulative to add each one to the ones before it.
  • A waterfall of the gains and then the losses of each result type adding up to your profit, with its table one click away. Tick % of Operative gains to see it all as a share of the gains of your first result type.

📉 Over time

  • Your equity in months of expenses at each month end, counted the same way. Tick or untick the loss result types that count as expenses, or tick Raw monthly averages to see their average monthly losses stacked instead.
  • A line showing your equity over time, nominal and real (dashed, deflated by the CPI to the prices of its last date). Hover over the line to see each day.

Initial movements are left out of the results here too, unless you tick Initial movements.

➕ Add a result type or base movement

Below the results are your result types and base movements. Open New result type or New base movement, fill in the form and press the button. Every entry needs a short name and a description.

  • A new base movement that is a gain or a loss also needs a result type.
  • For a base movement's debit and credit, choose an account to always use that account, or leave - to choose the account each time.
  • A new result type is added at the end of the list, right before the LAST one, which stays last. Tick Employment to tag it, which marks it with a badge.

To rename, reorder or remove entries, the settings files have to be edited by hand.
