🎯 Monthly budgets

Set a monthly budget for any loss base movement and compare it with what was spent in the month you pick, with what is left and what is over. A budget holds from its month on, until you change it, so past months keep theirs.

🐷 Saving goals

Saving goals track the balance of an asset account against a target in its currency, with what to save each month to reach it by its due date.

Nothing here adds movements to the ledger.
