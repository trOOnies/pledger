Your currencies: how prices rise in the base one, what the others are worth in it, and the conversions between them.

📈 Inflation and the price index

The inflation month by month of the base currency and the price index (CPI) built from it, day by day. The months that haven't ended yet are projected, and so are the estimated ones: dashed in the CPI chart and faded in the inflation one.

💵 The other currencies

  • What each one is worth in the base one over time, next to its real value: that value with inflation taken out, in today's prices. If it goes down, the currency grew less than prices.
  • The weekly values of each one, set with the New week form. An estimated one is the last known value, and a week that ends without a known value is warned about.
  • The balances of the accounts in each currency, and what the accounts in other currencies hold in their own currency.
  • How your assets are split by currency at each month end.

🔄 Conversions

Buying or selling another currency is a conversion: add it here, picking the currency sold and the one bought, with the amount in each one. Between two other currencies, it goes through the intermediary account Fi.

  • The Currency and Account filters narrow them down, and Initial movements adds the initial amounts of those accounts.
  • Each one shows the account's balance in its own currency right after it.

⚖️ Revaluations

Every time a currency's value changes, its accounts get a Foreign currency (G) or (L) gain or loss, so their balance always matches what they hold at that value. The Ledger shows them with a 💱.
