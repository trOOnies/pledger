What you hold in your investment accounts, and everything that happens to it.

📦 Holdings

  • Each investment with its type, quantity, amount, price (amount over quantity) and broker fees over its amount.
  • What your investments are made of, by type or by account.
  • Two charts show the total invested (nominal and real, dashed) and the total result, month by month.

📈 Buy or sell an investment

Use the form here, not the Ledger one. The investment results (capital gains and losses, interest, dividends, broker fees and stock splits) and the buying and selling of investments are added, edited and deleted here; the Ledger tab shows them with a 📈.

  • Name the investment and, when buying, selling or splitting, its quantity, so the amounts and quantities above stay up to date.
  • The money comes from (or goes to) another account, such as your bank account.
  • When buying or selling, or for a dividend, fill in Broker fees to add them in one go.
  • The Currency follows the account you pick: Pledger handles the conversion for you.

🏷️ Update prices

At the bottom: a new price for each investment held, all on one date. Once you enter one (by leaving its field, or pressing Enter), Change shows how much it moved from the last price: in green if up, in red if down, and a grey 0.00% if it did not change. The ones left blank are left out.

🏦 Interest on money

The interest earned or paid in a regular account with an interest frequency (set in the Accounts tab) is added by picking an Interest on Money base movement, with no investment. It gets a section of its own, by account and month by month, and counts towards the total result. It warns about each past week without interest of a weekly account, or each past month of a monthly one.

🔎 Movements

The Investment account, Investment and Base movement filters narrow the movements down (an investment shows all its movements, broker fees and dividends included). Each movement shows the total of its account and the amount and quantity of its investment right after it.
