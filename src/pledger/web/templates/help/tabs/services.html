Your services (rent, gas, internet…) and what you owe them.

🗓️ Monthly values

The monthly values of your services, a column each in the services file, are set month by month and split between the weeks of the month, on their Mondays (prorated, like the salaries of the Work tab). Each value becomes movements of its base_movement, an expense, crediting the account of its service, naming the service, up to today.

🏦 Their accounts

  • SeD: what you owe them.
  • SeL: what they owe you, for a service expected to be a debtor (one that charges in advance).

💸 New service payment

Pay them with the New service payment form. The value of the month, if known, fills in the amount; paying another one leaves the difference with the service, to balance later. If it isn't known, the amount paid can become the value of the month.

The movements of SeD and SeL are listed with what you owe each service after them, and the payments are deleted there. The Ledger tab shows them with a 🧾.
