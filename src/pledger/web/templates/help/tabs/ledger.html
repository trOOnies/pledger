Your movements, newest first, with the form to add new ones. It is the heart of pledger: every other tab reads from it (see Key ideas for what a movement is).

✍️ Record a movement

  1. Pick the date and the type: gain, loss or transfer.
  2. Pick the base movement that best describes it.
  3. Choose the debit and credit accounts, unless they are already filled in.
  4. Enter the amount, add a comment if you like, and press Add movement.

If something does not fit, for example an account that cannot be used with that base movement, Pledger explains the problem and nothing is saved. Not sure which accounts go where? See the Examples.

Bought with a credit card as the credit account? An Installments field shows up: leave it empty for a single payment, or write how many monthly installments it is in. The card is charged the whole amount that day all the same; the Credit Cards tab spreads what is paid of it, one installment a statement, by the card's statement closing dates.

Conversions, investments, debt movements, card payments and the rest are added in their own tabs, not here.

⧉ Repeat a movement

Click the ⧉ button at the end of the movement's row: the New movement form is filled in with it, date included. Change what you need, usually the date, and press Add movement. The original stays as it was, and the copy gets its own ID.

🩹 Fix a mistake

Click the ✎ button at the end of the movement's row: the form becomes Edit movement, filled in with it. Fix it and press Save changes, or Cancel to go back to a new movement. It keeps its ID. To remove it instead, use the × button.

🔎 Filters

The Month, Account and Base movement filters narrow everything down (a filter in use is highlighted). Pick All time to go back. The month is shared with the other tabs.

The movements setting each account's initial amount are left out unless you tick Initial movements.

🧾 Each movement

  • It shows the balance of its debit and credit accounts right after it.
  • It has an ID that never changes, given by pledger itself. Rows added to the CSVs by hand get theirs when pledger starts.

🔵 Movements of other tabs

Movements handled in another tab can't be edited or deleted here. Instead they show a colored circle linking to their row in that tab, or to the row they are generated from (like the month of a salary), highlighted:

  • 🟢 Investments
  • 🔵 Currencies: conversions and revaluations
  • 🟠 Work: salaries
  • 🟣 Accounts: initial amounts, and the balance corrections of the Audit tab, which can be deleted here
  • 🟡 People (debts and loans) and Services (what the services are owed, and their payments)
  • 🔴 Credit Cards: card payments
  • 🟤 Cart: the products of a cart

Their ID links there too, and the IDs of the Investments, Currencies, People, Services, Work and Credit Cards tabs link back to the same movement here.
