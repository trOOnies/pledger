Your work history, month by month, and what the companies you work for owe you.

🗓️ Each month

  • The company and job position.
  • The gross salary and its deductions (taxes, contributions… each a column of the work CSV, named after its loss base movement), which take it to the net salary.
  • The date you received the payment.

Months without a job are kept too, so no month is ever missing. Add the next month with the form (Fill in from copies the last month into it), or click ✎ on a month to edit it.

A table shows how this year's gross salary gets to the net one, and two charts show your gross and net salary over time, nominal and real (dashed).

🔁 How salaries reach the ledger

Each month's gross salary goes into the ledger week by week, split equally between the weeks of the month (Monday to Sunday, each in the month with at least 4 of its days), and so do its deductions, into Wo, the account of what the companies owe you: the net salary is owed by the company until it pays it. The Ledger tab shows these with a 🟠: change them by editing the month here.
