What you owe on each credit card, plus what was charged to it and paid off it. With a month picked, the balance is the one at the end of that month, and the charges and payments are the ones of that month.

📊 Charts

  • What was charged each month: your purchases, not your payments.
  • What is paid each month, with the purchases in installments split into them, the ones still due striped.
  • What you owe on them as a share of your equity, month by month.

🔎 Movements and installments

Their movements are listed below, each with what you owe on its card right after it (the Credit card filter shows the ones of one card), followed by the installments of the charges, picked by the month of their statement rather than by the date of the charge.

A purchase in installments is recorded in the Ledger (or a Cart), with the card as the credit account and the number of installments.

💳 Card payments

Card payments are added, edited and deleted here, picking the card and the account paying it. The Ledger tab shows them with a 🔴.

A card in another currency also shows what you owe in that currency, and its payments always say how much was paid in the card's currency, whatever currency you paid with.

📅 Statement closing dates

They are set here, in a table with a row for each month of a year and a column for each card. The months without one are autocompleted from the last one set before (the same day, as many months later), or are the last day of the month.

The charts of what is charged and paid each month follow them: a charge counts in the first statement closing on or after its date, and its installments in the statements after it, still due until each one closes. The results still count it in its own month.
