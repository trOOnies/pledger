Every account with its description and balance, your total assets, liabilities and equity, and the form to add a new account. See Key ideas for the three kinds of account.

📅 Balances and composition

  • With a month picked, balances show where each account stood at the end of that month.
  • What your assets and liabilities are made of, by currency or by liquidity.
  • At each month end, your debt over equity and your net liquidity (liquid assets minus liquid liabilities) over equity.

➕ Add an account

Fill in the New account form (name, type and description) and press Add account. Not sure it needs one? See Which kind of account? below.

  • Optionally, pick a check on its balance at the end of each day: Net positive (never negative) or Net zero (always 0.00, for an intermediary account). Movements that would break it are rejected, and the Checks column of the balances shows the accounts that have one.
  • An account that earns or pays interest on money needs its interest frequency: weekly or monthly. There is none by default, and only the accounts with one can have interest on money: the Investments tab lists them in its Interest on money section and warns about each past week, or month, without a movement of interest of them.

✎ Edit an account

Click ✎ on an asset or liability account to edit it: all but its name, type and currency.

🧭 Which kind of account?

Before adding an account, follow the questions from the top: the first Yes tells you what to use. Some answers are not a new account at all, but an entry of an account that already exists.

Is it something you own, or something you owe?

  1. 🟦 You own it: an asset
  2. Does it hold investments with a quantity and a price (bonds, stocks, funds, fixed terms…)? Yes → an investment account: an asset listed in investment.yaml, with its investments. Its initial amount comes from them.
  3. Is it money that someone owes you (a loan you gave)? Yes → no new account: add them to creditors.yaml, with a negative amount, and use the People tab: loans are in the loan accounts, one per currency.
  4. Is it only a step between movements that always goes back to 0.00? Yes → no new account: use Fi, the intermediary (net zero). Add one of your own only if you need it apart, with the Net zero check.
  5. None of the above (cash, a bank or wallet balance, a safe box…) → a regular asset, from this tab. Then: its currency, if not the base one; Liquid if it can be spent right away; Net positive if it can never go below 0.00; and its interest frequency if it earns interest.
  1. 🟧 You owe it: a liability
  2. Is it a credit card? Yes → a credit card account, in credit_cards.yaml: always liquid, paid from the Credit Cards tab, and its purchases can be in installments.
  3. Is it owed to a person or a service (a friend, the rent, a bill)? Yes → no new account: add them to creditors.yaml, with a positive amount: a person in people, with the People tab, where debts are in the debt accounts, one per currency; a service in services, with the Services tab, in SeD (or SeL). Your employers are there already, from the Work tab, in Wo.
  4. None of the above (a bank loan, a mortgage…) → a regular liability, from this tab, with its currency and Liquid if it is due right away.

There is no new equity account: Eq is the only one, and pledger uses it for every gain and loss.
