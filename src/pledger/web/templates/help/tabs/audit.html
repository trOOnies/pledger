Check your accounts against reality, from time to time, to catch a movement you forgot or got wrong.

🔍 Check your balances

  1. Fill in the balances you see in your bank or wallet without seeing pledger's, grouped into assets, investment accounts, debt, credit cards and other liabilities.
  2. Leave an account empty to skip it; a 0.00 is audited as zero. The net zero accounts, like Fi, are fixed at 0.00, and a net positive account can't be filled in as negative.
  3. Confirm, all at once: each balance is shown next to the actual one.

🩹 Differences

If one differs, find the mistake and fix it, or use the button next to it to add a Correction gain or loss dated today. It's optional: you can fix the difference yourself instead. The Ledger tab shows corrections with a 🔍, and they can be deleted there.

🗄️ History

Every audit you confirm is kept in the History, with its amounts and the corrections you accepted.
