Take your data with you.

📤 Export

Export data downloads all your files in one zip: a backup, or your data for pledger on another computer. Each data file can also be downloaded on its own.

📥 Import

Import such a zip to replace your data with its own, all of it.

  • It is only imported if it loads as a whole, so a zip pledger can't read changes nothing.
  • Your current data is saved to a backup first, listed here: import it to undo the import.
