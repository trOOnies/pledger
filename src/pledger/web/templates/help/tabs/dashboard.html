The home page: click the pledger logo, at the left of the top bar, to get here.

✍️ New movement

The New movement buttons take you to the tab and form of each kind of movement: a gain, loss or transfer in the Ledger, an investment, a conversion, a debt or loan, a card payment, and so on.

🧩 Cards and plots

  • Next to them are slots for number cards and plots: click a dotted one with a + to pick what goes in it, and the × of a card or plot to free its slot.
  • Rearrange lets you drag them to other slots, or move them with their arrows. Click Done, press Escape or leave the tab to stop.
  • Click the title of a card or plot to open the tab it comes from.
