Take a get-together with up to 7 more people to the ledger, in three steps. Nothing is written before the last one.

1️⃣ What was meant vs What was paid

One table, a column per person, named in its header (+ adds one, the × of its header removes it), and a row per item with its price and the base movement of your share.

  • Check who fronts each item, split evenly among them, or turn on Percentages to give each one a share of it (adding up to 100%).
  • The last row is what each one actually put in.
  • Split says who pays whom so that everyone is even.

2️⃣ Settling differences

The date, the account you paid from, and for each one you owe or who owes you, what is paid now (0.00 by default) and into or out of which account. The rest stays as a debt or loan in the People tab. Each one is shown as already in People or to be added.

3️⃣ Reflect with movements

Process all lists the movements to check. Create movements creates them and adds the new people, or Return to Settling goes back.
