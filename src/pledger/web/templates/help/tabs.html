Every tab works on the same ledger: the Ledger tab lists all its movements, and the others each look at one part of it, add the movements of that part with a form of their own, or both. Each tab has a page here: pick one below, or read them in order with the links at the bottom.

The top bar groups them like here: Info, Revenue, Debt and Other unroll when you point at them, and the rest are always in sight. The Dashboard is the pledger logo, at the left.

🧩 Shared by every tab

  • The Month filter is shared: pick a month on Ledger, Accounts, Results, People or Credit Cards and the other tabs open on that same month. Pick All time to clear it. A filter in use is highlighted.
  • A movement belongs to the tab that adds it. The Ledger shows it with the emoji of that tab linking back to it, where it is edited and deleted (see Ledger).
  • Most tables sort by any of their columns: click its header, and again to reverse the order.
  • The eye, theme and Save & Quit buttons are at the top right of every tab (see Getting started).
