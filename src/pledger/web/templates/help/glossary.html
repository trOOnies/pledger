Balance
How much an account holds (assets) or how much is owed on it (liabilities) at a point in time, after adding up all its movements.
Base currency
The currency every amount in Pledger is written in.
Base movement
The category of a movement, which says if it is a gain, a loss or a transfer (see Key ideas, and the Results tab).
Conversion
Buying or selling another currency, from the Currencies tab.
CPI (price index)
A number that follows how prices rise in your base currency, built day by day from the monthly inflation. It starts at 100.
Cumulative
In the result table of the Results tab, the net results added up from the first result type down to that row. The last row equals your profit.
Debt and loan accounts
The accounts of what you owe people (debt) and what people owe you (loan), two per currency, followed person by person in the People tab.
Employment
A tag for the result types whose gains and losses only happen while you have a job, like your salary (e.g. a Work result type) and the taxes taken from it (e.g. Work Taxes). Their losses aren't counted as expenses in the months of expenses of the Results tab and the Dashboard, since they would stop with the job. Tag a result type with employment: true in the base movements file, or tick Employment when adding it in the Results tab, which marks it with a badge.
Equity
Your net worth: assets minus liabilities. Gains raise it and losses lower it.
Fixed side
A debit or credit account that a base movement always uses.
Gains, losses and profit
The total of your gain movements, the total of your loss movements, and the difference between them, for the chosen period.
Initial movement
The movement that sets an account's starting amount. Pledger creates it on its own, so you never add it.
Installments
A credit card purchase paid in parts, one per statement (see Credit Cards).
Intermediary account
Fi: an account that is only a step between movements and always goes back to 0.00, like a conversion between two other currencies.
Liquid
Money you can use right away, such as cash or a checking account, unlike a fixed-term deposit.
Movement
One entry in the ledger: money moving from one account to another on a date.
Net
Gains minus losses, here for a single result type.
Net zero, Net positive
The checks an account can have on its balance at the end of each day: always 0.00, or never negative (see Accounts).
Price
What one unit of an investment cost on average: its amount divided by its quantity.
Real value
A value with inflation taken out, in today's prices, so you can compare amounts from different times fairly.
Result type
A family of gains and losses, such as Operative or Taxes (see Key ideas, and the Results tab).
Revaluation
The Foreign currency gain or loss of an account in another currency when that currency's value changes (see Currencies).
Statement closing date
The day a credit card's statement closes: the charges up to it are paid in that statement (see Credit Cards).
