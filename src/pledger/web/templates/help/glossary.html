Balance
How much an account holds (assets) or how much is owed on it (liabilities) at a point in time, after adding up all its movements.
Base currency
The currency every amount in Pledger is written in.
CPI (price index)
A number that follows how prices rise in your base currency, built day by day from the monthly inflation. It starts at 100.
Cumulative
In the result table of the Results tab, the net results added up from the first result type down to that row. The last row equals your profit.
Equity
Your net worth: assets minus liabilities. Gains raise it and losses lower it.
Fixed side
A debit or credit account that a base movement always uses.
Gains, losses and profit
The total of your gain movements, the total of your loss movements, and the difference between them, for the chosen period.
Initial movement
The movement that sets an account's starting amount. Pledger creates it on its own, so you never add it.
Liquid
Money you can use right away, such as cash or a checking account, unlike a fixed-term deposit.
Movement
One entry in the ledger: money moving from one account to another on a date.
Net
Gains minus losses, here for a single result type.
Price
What one unit of an investment cost on average: its amount divided by its quantity.
Real value
A value with inflation taken out, in today's prices, so you can compare amounts from different times fairly.
