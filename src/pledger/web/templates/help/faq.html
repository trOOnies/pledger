🤔 Why does my profit differ from the change in my bank balance?

Profit only counts gains and losses. Transfers, such as moving money to savings or paying a credit card bill, change account balances but not your profit.

🔒 Why can't I choose the equity account?

Pledger uses equity on its own: it is the credit side of every gain and the debit side of every loss, and transfers never touch it.

⚠️ The page says it could not load the ledger.

One of the settings files or the ledger has a problem, for example a movement that uses an account or base movement that no longer exists. The message says what is wrong; fix that entry in the file and reload the page.
