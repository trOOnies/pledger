Pledger is a personal ledger: a list of every time money moved, where it came from and where it went. From that list it works out how much you have in each account, how much you earned and spent, and how your wealth changes over time.

Each time money moves you add one movement: a date, what kind of movement it was, the two accounts involved and the amount. Everything else in Pledger is calculated from those movements.

💱 All amounts are in your base currency ({{ settings.currency }}). If you hold other currencies, enter their value converted to the base currency (but the initial amounts of the accounts, which are in their own currency).

👁️ Showing Pledger to someone? The eye button at the top right replaces every amount with ****** until you click it again.

⏻ Done for now? Save & Quit, at the top right, saves what is left to save and stops Pledger (the make serve server too), closing the window when the browser allows it.
